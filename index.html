The Art of Sharpening

Precision, Edge Care, and True Metallurgy.

Mechanical grinders generate aggressive friction that can ruin steel temper. We hone strictly by hand on progressive waterstones, removing only the microscopic layer needed to establish a clean apex.

Every blade receives individualized attention: bevel angles are measured to factory specifications, secondary micro-bevels add edge durability, and leather stropping eliminates microscopic burrs.

Cold Honing Water-cooled slurry protects factory heat treatment.
Angle Integrity Exact angle calibration for clean food separation.

Workshop Discipline

The Craftsmanship Difference.

We treat sharpening as metallurgical stewardship rather than high-speed commodity grinding.

01

Careful Inspection

Before sharpening, we evaluate spine straightness, heel wear, edge chips, and steel hardness under direct task lighting.

02

Appropriate Sharpening Methods

Western blades, Japanese single-bevels, and serrated knives receive distinct stone progressions matched to their metallurgy.

03

Transparent Estimates

All sharpening fees and necessary repair costs are clearly communicated before any stone touches your steel.

04

Responsible Handling

Knives remain housed in protective guards, safely stored on designated benches, and handed back ready for immediate culinary use.
