Step-by-Step

How Drop-Offs Are Arranged

We keep the intake process straightforward so you always know when your knives will be worked on:

  1. 1
    Submit an Enquiry: Share your knife type and service requirements using the form below.
  2. 2
    Await Confirmation: We review our workbench schedule and contact you to confirm a mutually convenient drop-off arrangement.
  3. 3
    Hand Off Safely: Bring your blades wrapped securely as agreed. We inspect the edges and begin honing.
Note: Please do not leave cutlery unattended without prior coordination.
Safe Transport

Packaging Your Knives Safely

Protect yourself and your edges during transit with these simple packaging best practices:

  1. 1
    Fitted Sayas or Blade Guards: If you have wooden sayas or plastic edge guards, slip them on before transport.
  2. 2
    Towel or Cardboard Wrap: If guards are unavailable, wrap each blade tightly in a clean kitchen towel or folded cardboard, securing with tape.
  3. 3
    Sturdy Carrier: Place wrapped knives into a knife roll, tote bag, or shallow box so tips cannot puncture through.
We provide sanitized transport covers for all completed sharpenings.

Assessment Guide

How to Photograph Damage

Clear photos ensure we can quote chip repairs and edge reprofiling accurately:

  • •
    Side Profile on Plain Background: Lay the knife flat on a cutting board or sheet of paper to show the full belly curve.
  • •
    Close-Up of the Chip: Take a clear photo directly over any chipped or notched section of the cutting edge.
  • •
    Spine Down Inspection: Looking down from the spine helps evaluate blade straightness and secondary thickness.
Photos can also be shown directly on your phone during your coordinated drop-off visit.
