The Art of the True Apex

A knife is an extension of the cook's hand. Ferrum & Stone was founded to preserve culinary blades through traditional Japanese waterstones, disciplined geometry, and zero heat damage.

100% Waterstone • Zero Belt Friction
Our Guiding Principle

Why High-Speed Grinders Ruin Good Knives

Industrial sharpening services often rely on dry motorized belt grinders. In seconds, friction temperatures at the fragile cutting apex spike beyond 400°F, destroying the factory heat-treat temper and leaving the steel soft.

“Once steel loses its temper at the apex, it will roll after a single prep shift. We hone slowly on waterstones where cool water carries away heat and debris simultaneously.”

By sharpening exclusively by hand, we preserve the microscopic carbide structure of carbon steel and high-end stainless, ensuring an edge that bites cleanly and lasts months between touch-ups.

Artisan Standards

Our Four Bench Disciplines

Every blade passing across our workbench is treated with metallurgical precision.

DISCIPLINE 01 Curvature Integrity

Anatomical Edge Geometry

We reset apex angles strictly matched to the steel and blade use: 12° for delicate Kataba slicers, 15° for Japanese Santoku, and 18°–20° for heavy European chef knives. We never flatten out a curved rocking belly.

Primary Apex: 12° – 20° Tolerance: ±0.5°
DISCIPLINE 02 Slurry Dynamics

Whetstone Progression

From 400-grit coarse shaping stones through 2000-grit refining to 8000-grit polishing stones, we manage stone slurry to achieve an acute apex without gouging the blade flanks.

Progression: 400# → 8000# Lubricant: Filtered Spring Water
DISCIPLINE 03 Apex Cleanliness

Micro-Burr Elimination

A sharp edge that still carries a microscopic wire burr will dull instantly. We remove burrs completely using progressive edge-leading waterstone passes and unconditioned leather strops.

Burr Detection: 10x Loupe Optics Substrate: Russian Bridle Leather
DISCIPLINE 04 Organic Care

Timber & Handle Stabilization

Cutlery care extends beyond the blade. We re-tighten loose brass pins, buff bolsters, and seal dried wooden scales using non-toxic food-safe organic beeswax and pure camellia oil.

Sealant: Pure Camellia Oil Food Safe: 100% Organic Grade
The Science of Abrasives

The Japanese Waterstone Hierarchy

Understanding the progression of natural and synthetic stones used at our workbench.

荒砥 Arato 220–600#

Arato (Coarse Shaping)

Used strictly for rapid stock removal, repairing deep chips, straightening bent tips, and wide-bevel thinning without thermal shock.

Application Reprofiling • Stock Removal
中砥 Nakato 1000–2000#

Nakato (Medium Refining)

Sets the primary cutting bevel, removes coarse scratches, and establishes the foundational apex bite and tooth required for culinary slicing.

Application Foundational Cutting Bevel
仕上砥 Shiageto 4000–8000#

Shiageto (Finishing & Polish)

Polishes the bevel to a mirror sheen, aligns micro-teeth, and produces a surgical hair-shaving edge for delicate proteins and sushi.

Application Mirror Apex • Surgical Edge
合砥 Awasedo Natural Strop

Awasedo & Leather Strop

Final deburring pass on natural horsehide charged with fine sub-micron diamond emulsion for frictionless, drag-free cutting glide.

Application Micro-Burr Removal • Glide
